# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Membership Page Messaging Architecture v3**

## **Purpose**

The Membership Page exists to answer one question:

### **Why should our organisation join the First Nations Action Network?**

The page should clearly communicate the practical, strategic and community benefits of participation.

Membership is not a software subscription.

Membership is participation in a growing national network that helps organisations connect, engage, amplify their work and strengthen community impact.

# **Core Promise**

## **Join A National Network That Helps Your Organisation Connect, Grow And Amplify Its Impact**

The First Nations Action Network helps organisations increase visibility, strengthen participation, build relationships and access the tools, resources and support needed to create positive change.

Membership provides access to a growing ecosystem of organisations working together across Australia.

# **The Membership Transformation**

## **Before Membership**

Many organisations experience:

-   Limited visibility
-   Limited reach
-   Volunteer burnout
-   Resource constraints
-   Difficulty sustaining engagement
-   Isolation from similar organisations

## **After Membership**

Members gain access to:

-   A national network
-   Community organising infrastructure
-   Content amplification opportunities
-   Community directory visibility
-   Shared resources and learning
-   New collaboration opportunities

Membership helps organisations strengthen their capacity while increasing their reach and impact.
