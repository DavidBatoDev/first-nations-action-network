User Journey Considerations
The Who We Are page should move visitors from understanding FNAN's beliefs to trusting its people and governance, ultimately encouraging deeper engagement.
Design Principles
• Authenticity over polish
• Community before organisation
• Story before statistics
• Clarity and accessibility
• Cultural respect and representation
