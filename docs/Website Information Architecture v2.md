# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Website Information Architecture v2**

## **Website Purpose**

The website serves four primary objectives:

### **1\. Grow the Network**

Encourage organisations to become members of the First Nations Action Network.

### **2\. Generate Training & Development Opportunities**

Promote workshops, learning programs and engagement opportunities.

### **3\. Showcase the Network**

Demonstrate the breadth, diversity and impact of organisations participating across Australia.

### **4\. Build Trust and Credibility**

Provide visibility into the mission, leadership, members, events and activities of the network.

# **PRIMARY NAVIGATION**

## **Home**

Overview of the network, mission and opportunities to participate.

## **Membership**

Information about joining the network, membership benefits and onboarding.

Primary conversion page.

## **Training & Development**

Workshops, learning opportunities and organisational capacity building services.

Primary revenue page.

## **Community Directory**

Interactive directory of participating organisations across Australia.

Examples may include:

-   First Nations organisations
-   Ally organisations
-   Reconciliation groups
-   Community groups
-   Advocacy organisations
-   Cultural organisations
-   Workplace networks
-   Social enterprises

Purpose:

To demonstrate the strength and reach of the network while helping members discover and connect with one another.

## **Resources**

Articles, guides, toolkits, templates, educational content and shared learning resources.

## **Events**

Forums, workshops, community events, webinars and gatherings.

## **About**

Mission, vision, story, leadership, governance and network structure.

## **Contact**

General enquiries, membership conversations and partnership opportunities.

# **KEY STRATEGIC SHIFT**

The website is not organised around different membership categories.

The website is organised around one national network.

Organisations may participate in different ways, but all belong to the same ecosystem and share the same overarching mission of strengthening communities and creating positive change.
