# **PRIMARY MEMBERSHIP OFFER**

## **Network Membership**

Network Membership is designed for organisations that are committed to strengthening communities, building relationships and creating positive change.

Membership is organisation-based rather than individual-based and provides access to the First Nations Action Network ecosystem, including community organising infrastructure, training, resources, collaboration opportunities and national connections.

### **Who Membership Is For**

Membership is open to a diverse range of organisations and community initiatives, including:

-   First Nations organisations
-   Ally organisations
-   Reconciliation groups
-   Community groups
-   Advocacy organisations
-   Cultural organisations
-   Workplace networks
-   Social enterprises
-   Community-led projects

The First Nations Action Network recognises that meaningful change requires collaboration across many different communities and sectors.

### **Membership Outcomes**

Membership is designed to help organisations:

-   Build stronger community relationships
-   Improve communication and engagement
-   Develop local leadership
-   Coordinate volunteers and supporters
-   Deliver events and campaigns more effectively
-   Access shared resources and learning opportunities
-   Connect with organisations across Australia
-   Participate in a growing national network

### **Membership Includes**

#### **National Network Access**

Connection to a growing network of organisations working towards positive community outcomes.

#### **Community Organising Infrastructure**

Access to systems and tools that support communication, engagement, events, campaigns and volunteer coordination.

#### **Learning and Development**

Onboarding, training, educational resources and community organising support.

#### **Resource Library**

Templates, guides, community resources and shared learning materials.

#### **Community Visibility**

Opportunities to share stories, promote initiatives and participate in collaborative activities.

#### **State and Regional Connections**

Access to local and regional networks that strengthen relationships and support collaboration.

### **Membership Investment**

$1,200 annually

or

$150 monthly

Membership is held by the organisation and may include multiple team members operating under a designated Captain or primary contact.
