## **Building Stronger Communities Through Connection, Collaboration and Action**

# **Welcome**

The First Nations Action Network is a growing national network of organisations working together to strengthen communities, develop leadership and create positive change.

We bring together First Nations organisations, ally organisations, reconciliation groups, advocacy organisations, community groups and social enterprises that share a commitment to community wellbeing, collaboration and meaningful action.

Membership provides access to a national network, community organising support, shared resources, leadership development opportunities and community engagement infrastructure designed to help organisations increase their impact.

# **Our Vision**

A community built on a foundation of compassion where the inherent rights of First Nations peoples to self-determination, self-autonomy and economic independence are fully embraced through every action.

We are dedicated to mutually empowering one another and strengthening our collective community.

# **Our Mission**

To nurture authentic relationships and organise communities through progressive collaboration, driving leadership for change and advocating for community wellbeing.

# **Why Join The First Nations Action Network?**

Many organisations are doing important work in their communities.

However, they often face common challenges:

-   Limited resources
-   Volunteer burnout
-   Community disengagement
-   Isolation from other organisations
-   Limited access to training and support
-   Difficulty sustaining momentum

The First Nations Action Network exists to help organisations overcome these challenges by creating opportunities for collaboration, shared learning and collective action.

Membership helps organisations strengthen their capacity while remaining independent and community-led.

# **Who Membership Is For**

Membership is open to organisations and community initiatives that share a commitment to strengthening communities and creating positive change.

Examples include:

-   First Nations organisations
