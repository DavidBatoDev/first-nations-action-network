# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Member Portal Blueprint v1**

## **Purpose**

The Member Portal serves as the central access point for paid members of the First Nations Action Network.

It provides members with access to:

-   Membership benefits
-   Resources
-   Community opportunities
-   Story submissions
-   Event submissions
-   Community organising infrastructure
-   Future platform functionality

The portal becomes the digital home for participating organisations.

# **Portal Vision**

## **One Login. One Community. One Network.**

Members should not need to navigate multiple disconnected systems.

The portal should act as the primary member experience regardless of what tools are operating behind the scenes.

Current infrastructure may use Action Network and Action Builder.

Future infrastructure may use a custom First Nations Action Platform.

The portal remains the member-facing gateway.

# **Strategic Vision**

The Member Portal is the digital home of the First Nations Action Network.

It provides organisations with a single place to access resources, participate in the network, share stories, promote events and engage with the broader community.

As technology evolves, the portal remains the central experience that connects organisations to opportunities, resources and relationships across Australia.
