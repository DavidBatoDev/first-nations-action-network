# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Membership Benefits Architecture v1**

## **Purpose**

This document defines the core benefits of Network Membership and explains the value members receive through participation in the First Nations Action Network.

The objective is to clearly articulate why organisations choose to join and remain active members of the network.

# **Core Membership Promise**

## **Join A National Network That Helps Your Organisation Connect, Grow and Amplify Its Impact**

Membership provides organisations with access to relationships, visibility, resources, support and community organising infrastructure that helps strengthen participation and create positive change.

Members are not purchasing software.

Members are joining a growing national ecosystem of organisations working together to strengthen communities across Australia.

# **The Five Membership Benefit Pillars**

## **Pillar 1**

# **Network Access**

Membership connects organisations to a growing national network of:

-   First Nations organisations
-   Ally organisations
-   Reconciliation groups
-   Community groups
-   Advocacy organisations
-   Cultural organisations
-   Workplace networks
-   Social enterprises

### **Benefits**

-   Build relationships across Australia
-   Learn from other organisations
-   Discover collaboration opportunities
-   Access peer support
-   Reduce organisational isolation

### **Outcome**

Your organisation becomes part of something bigger than itself.

## **Pillar 2**

# **Visibility & Amplification**

One of the most valuable benefits of membership is increased visibility across the network.

Members gain opportunities to share:

-   Community stories
-   Events
-   Campaigns
-   News
-   Initiatives
-   Success stories

### **Story Publishing**

Members may submit stories for publication through the First Nations Action Network website.

Examples:

-   Community achievements
-   Opinion pieces
-   Event reports
-   Community initiatives
-   Campaign updates

### **Event Promotion**

Members may submit events for promotion through the network.

Examples:

-   Community events
-   Workshops
-   Forums
-   Gatherings
-   Webinars

### **Content Amplification**

Approved content may be featured through network communication channels and made available for distribution across the ecosystem.

This increases exposure and helps organisations reach audiences beyond their immediate community.

### **Outcome**

Your stories, events and initiatives reach more people.

## **Pillar 3**

# **Community Organising Infrastructure**

Membership includes access to systems that support community engagement and participation.

Current infrastructure includes:

-   Action Network
-   Action Builder

Future infrastructure will evolve as the network develops its own member platform and CRM.

### **Supported Activities**

-   Email communications
-   Event management
-   Campaign management
-   Surveys
-   Forms
-   Community engagement
-   Volunteer coordination

### **Important Principle**

Members are not purchasing access to Action Network.

Members are receiving access to community organising infrastructure provided by the First Nations Action Network.

The underlying technology may evolve over time.

### **Outcome**

Your organisation gains access to tools that support engagement, participation and action.

## **Pillar 4**

# **Community Directory Visibility**

Members gain visibility through participation in a national organisation directory.

The directory will become one of Australia's most comprehensive collections of First Nations and community-focused organisations.

### **Benefits**

-   Increased discoverability
-   Improved online visibility
-   Greater credibility
-   Connection opportunities
-   Referral opportunities

### **Potential Directory Information**

-   Organisation name
-   Description
-   Location
-   Website
-   Focus areas
-   Organisation type

### **Outcome**

People can find and connect with your organisation more easily.

## **Pillar 5**

# **Resources & Learning**

Membership provides access to educational resources and practical support.

### **Included**

-   Onboarding support
-   Platform training
-   Community organising guidance
-   Resource library
-   Templates
-   Guides
-   Best practices

### **Onboarding Support**

Members receive support to learn how to use the network's community organising infrastructure.

Topics may include:

-   Email communications
-   Campaign creation
-   Event management
-   Surveys and forms
-   Community engagement workflows

### **Important Clarification**

Membership onboarding and platform training are included.

Professional training and development services are separate offerings.

### **Outcome**

Members gain the knowledge required to effectively use available tools and opportunities.

# **What Membership Is Not**

Membership is not:

-   Software licensing
-   Generic directory listing
-   Advertising
-   Consulting services
-   Professional training packages

Membership is participation in a national network designed to strengthen organisations and communities.

# **Strategic Positioning Statement**

The First Nations Action Network helps organisations connect, engage and amplify their impact through a growing national network, community organising infrastructure, content amplification opportunities and shared learning.

Membership is designed to strengthen organisations so they can strengthen communities.
