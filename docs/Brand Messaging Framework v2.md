# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Messaging Framework v2**

## **Brand Positioning**

### **What Is The First Nations Action Network?**

The First Nations Action Network is a national network of organisations, community leaders and changemakers working together to strengthen communities, develop leadership and create positive change.

The network supports collaboration, community organising, learning, resource sharing and collective action across Australia.

# **Core Brand Idea**

## **Strong Communities Create Lasting Change**

The First Nations Action Network exists to help organisations connect, collaborate and build stronger communities.

The network believes that lasting change happens when people work together, share knowledge and support one another.

# **Core Message**

### **We help organisations connect, organise and take action together.**

This message should remain consistent across the website, membership materials, presentations and future platform.

# **The Big Promise**

Membership in the First Nations Action Network means your organisation does not have to do this work alone.

Members gain access to a growing national network, shared resources, leadership support and community organising infrastructure that helps strengthen local impact.

# **Homepage Headline**

### **Building Stronger Communities Through Connection, Collaboration and Action**

Supporting Statement:

The First Nations Action Network brings together organisations across Australia to strengthen communities, develop leadership, share resources and create positive change through collective action.

# **Membership Headline**

### **Join a National Network Building Stronger Communities**

Supporting Statement:

Membership provides access to community organising support, leadership development, shared resources and collaboration opportunities designed to help organisations increase their impact.

# **Training & Development Headline**

### **Learning Through Experience. Leading Through Understanding.**

Supporting Statement:

Support your organisation, team or community through practical learning, cultural engagement and leadership development.

# **Primary Audiences**

## **Member Organisations**

Organisations seeking support, connection, collaboration and community organising capacity.

Examples include:

-   First Nations organisations
-   Ally organisations
-   Reconciliation groups
-   Community groups
-   Advocacy organisations
-   Cultural organisations
-   Workplace networks
-   Social enterprises

## **Institutions & Partners**

Government agencies, councils, educational institutions, businesses and other organisations seeking to engage respectfully and effectively with First Nations communities.

## **Supporters & Community Members**

Individuals who wish to participate in events, access resources, support campaigns, purchase merchandise or contribute to the growth of the network.

# **Language To Avoid**

Avoid overly technical language when speaking to prospective members.

Do not lead with:

-   CRM
-   Database
-   Software platform
-   User licences
-   Seats
-   Automation
-   Technology stack

Technology supports the mission but is not the mission itself.

# **Membership Transformation**

Before Membership:

-   Working in isolation
-   Limited resources
-   Limited support
-   Limited visibility
-   Volunteer burnout
-   Difficulty maintaining momentum

After Membership:

-   Connected to a national network
-   Access to support and shared learning
-   Improved communication and engagement
-   Stronger leadership development
-   Greater collaboration opportunities
-   Increased community impact

# **Elevator Pitch**

### **Short Version**

The First Nations Action Network is a national network of organisations working together to strengthen communities through collaboration, leadership development, community organising and shared action.

### **Extended Version**

The First Nations Action Network supports organisations across Australia by providing community organising infrastructure, leadership development opportunities, shared resources and national collaboration. Through connection, learning and collective action, members are able to strengthen community participation and create lasting positive change.

# **Strategic Messaging Principle**

The First Nations Action Network is not a software platform.

The First Nations Action Network is not a collection of individual groups.

The First Nations Action Network is a growing national network of organisations working together to strengthen communities and create positive change.

Everything else exists to support that mission.
