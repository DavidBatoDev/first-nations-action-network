# **FIRST NATIONS ACTION NETWORK**

# **Membership Product Blueprint v2**

## **Purpose**

This document defines:

-   What membership is
-   What members receive
-   How membership operates
-   How technology supports membership
-   How future platform development should evolve

This blueprint serves as the foundation for:

-   Website development
-   Membership onboarding
-   CRM implementation
-   Future software platform development
-   Operational procedures

# **Guiding Principle**

## **Members Join A Network, Not A Software Platform**

Technology exists to support relationships, participation and community action.

The network is the product.

The platform is the infrastructure.

Community outcomes are the goal.

# **Core Entity Structure**

The First Nations Action Network is built around organisations.

Not individual users.

Not software accounts.

Not membership categories.

The organisation is the primary entity.

## **Level 1**

### **First Nations Action Network**

National organisation responsible for:

-   National coordination
-   Membership management
-   Platform management
-   Resource development
-   Training programs
-   State coordinator support
-   Network growth

## **Level 2**

### **State Networks**

Examples:

-   Queensland
-   New South Wales
-   Victoria
-   South Australia
-   Western Australia
-   Tasmania
-   Northern Territory
-   ACT

Responsibilities:

-   Regional relationship building
-   Member support
-   Community development
-   Events and engagement
-   Local collaboration

## **Level 3**

### **Member Organisations**

Member organisations form the foundation of the network.

Examples include:

-   First Nations organisations
-   Ally organisations
-   Reconciliation groups
-   Community groups
-   Advocacy organisations
-   Cultural organisations
-   Workplace networks
-   Social enterprises
-   Community-led projects

Membership belongs to the organisation.

## **Level 4**

### **Organisation Members**

Users within an organisation.

Examples:

-   Captain
-   Communications Lead
-   Volunteer Coordinator
-   Events Coordinator
-   Leadership Team Members

Users belong to organisations.

Organisations belong to the network.

# **Network Membership**

## **Overview**

Network Membership is the primary membership offering of the First Nations Action Network.

Membership provides organisations with access to:

-   Community organising infrastructure
-   Learning opportunities
-   Shared resources
-   National connections
-   Collaboration opportunities
-   Community engagement support

Membership is organisation-based rather than individual-based.

## **Membership Investment**

### **Annual Membership**

$1,200 per year

or

### **Monthly Membership**

$150 per month

# **Membership Outcomes**

Membership is designed to help organisations:

### **Build Stronger Community Relationships**

Strengthen connections with community members, supporters and stakeholders.

### **Improve Community Communication**

Engage communities more effectively through coordinated communications and outreach.

### **Increase Participation**

Encourage greater involvement from volunteers, supporters and community leaders.

### **Develop Leadership**

Support leadership growth within organisations and communities.

### **Coordinate Community Action**

Deliver campaigns, events, consultations and initiatives more effectively.

### **Connect With A National Network**

Build relationships with organisations across Australia working toward similar goals.

# **Membership Components**

Network Membership consists of five core pillars.

## **Pillar 1**

### **Network**

Members gain access to:

-   National connections
-   State networks
-   Collaboration opportunities
-   Shared learning
-   Community support

Purpose:

Reduce isolation and strengthen relationships.

## **Pillar 2**

### **Community Organising Infrastructure**

Members gain access to systems that support:

-   Community engagement
-   Supporter management
-   Communications
-   Events
-   Campaigns
-   Volunteer coordination
-   Participation tracking

Current infrastructure is supported through:

-   Action Network
-   Action Builder

Future infrastructure may be delivered through a dedicated First Nations Action Network platform.

The technology may change.

The member experience should remain consistent.

## **Pillar 3**

### **Learning**

Members gain access to:

-   Onboarding
-   Educational resources
-   Training opportunities
-   Community organising guidance
-   Leadership development opportunities

Purpose:

Build organisational capacity.

## **Pillar 4**

### **Visibility**

Members gain opportunities to:

-   Promote initiatives
-   Share community stories
-   Participate in collaborative projects
-   Showcase achievements
-   Increase community awareness

Purpose:

Strengthen community recognition and engagement.

## **Pillar 5**

### **Community**

Members become part of a broader ecosystem of organisations, leaders and communities working together to create positive change.

Purpose:

Strengthen belonging, collaboration and collective impact.

# **Organisation Types**

The First Nations Action Network is built around organisations rather than membership categories.

Organisations may identify with one or more organisation types.

Examples include:

### **First Nations Organisations**

Organisations led by and serving First Nations communities.

### **Ally Organisations**

Groups supporting positive outcomes for First Nations peoples through learning, advocacy, relationship building and community participation.

### **Reconciliation Groups**

Groups focused on truth-telling, reconciliation and community engagement.

### **Community Groups**

Locally based organisations serving specific communities or regions.

### **Advocacy Organisations**

Groups focused on awareness, engagement and policy outcomes.

### **Cultural Organisations**

Groups supporting culture, education, knowledge sharing and community wellbeing.

### **Workplace Networks**

Employee-led or workplace-based groups supporting positive community outcomes.

### **Social Enterprises**

Mission-driven organisations creating social and community impact.

# **Organisation Structure**

Each member organisation receives an organisational profile.

## **Organisation Profile**

Potential fields include:

-   Organisation Name
-   Description
-   Logo
-   Website
-   State
-   Region
-   Organisation Type
-   Focus Areas
-   Contact Information

## **Captain**

Every organisation has one designated Captain.

The Captain serves as:

-   Primary contact
-   Membership administrator
-   Team manager
-   Training coordinator

Responsibilities include:

-   Maintaining organisational information
-   Managing team access
-   Coordinating onboarding
-   Representing the organisation within the network

## **Team Members**

Additional users may be added under the organisation.

Examples include:

-   Communications staff
-   Volunteer coordinators
-   Event coordinators
-   Leadership team members

Initial allocation may include up to five users.

This can evolve as the network grows.

# **Critical Rule**

The website database must remain the source of truth.

The website should own:

-   Organisations
-   Memberships
-   Plans
-   Renewal dates
-   Team members
-   Organisation types
-   States
-   Membership status

This protects future migration and platform independence.

# **Product Vision Statement**

The First Nations Action Network provides organisations with the relationships, resources, leadership support and community organising infrastructure needed to strengthen communities and create lasting positive change.

Membership connects organisations to a growing national network that helps them build capacity, increase participation and create greater impact in their communities.
